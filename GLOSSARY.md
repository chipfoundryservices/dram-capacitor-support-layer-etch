# Glossary: DRAM Capacitor Support Layer Etch

Terms are defined as they are used in this book. Chapter references point to the main discussion.

---

## A

**ACL (amorphous carbon layer):** The carbon hard mask of the support-open pattern, 300 nm in S0 and S1 and 220 nm in S2 with SiCN supports; consumed 253 nm of 300 nm in the one-pass route. (Ch. 4.3, 6.2, 14.1)

**Actinometry:** Normalizing an emission line by the Ar 750.4 nm line to remove changes in electron density and temperature. (Ch. 8.2.3)

**ALE (atomic-layer etching):** Etching by alternating self-limiting modification (CH₃F/Ar) and removal (Ar⁺ at 35 eV) steps; 0.5 nm per 5.5 s cycle on nitride. (Ch. 7.3)

**ARDE (aspect-ratio-dependent etching):** Fall of etch rate with aspect ratio, ER/ER₀ = 1/(1 + kA), k = 0.02. (Ch. 3.6.2)

**Array edge:** The boundary of a cell array block, where lattice completeness and pillar loading change. (Ch. 10)

---

## B

**Bare rate:** The etch rate of a surface with no polymer film at a given ion energy; 1730 nm/min for SiN, 493 for SiO₂, 23 for TiN at 400 eV. (Ch. 3.3.1)

**BPSG:** Borophosphosilicate glass; the lower mold oxide, HF rate 600 nm/min. (Ch. 2.3.1, App. A.3)

**Bottom stop:** The 20 nm LPCVD nitride at the landing-pad level; never opened. (Ch. 1.1.2)

**Breakthrough (BT):** A short high-energy step that removes the damaged top layer; not usable on bare TiN here because it would cost 1.5 nm in 4 s. (Ch. 2.4.1)

---

## C

**Cassie relation:** cos θ = φ cos θ_polymer + (1 − φ) cos θ_surface; the apparent contact angle of a wall partly covered by polymer. (Ch. 9.4.2)

**CCP (capacitively coupled plasma):** The dual-frequency chamber type (60 MHz source, 2 MHz bias) used for the support layer etch. (Ch. 5)

**Chamber memory:** The influence of the walls' polymer state, left by the previous step, on the next; resets with the flash. (Ch. 5.3)

**Class (of a cell):** Touched (the pillar overlaps an opening) or untouched (it does not). 75% / 25% for the reference lattice. (Ch. 1.2.4, 11.5)

**Class signature:** The excess of failing cells on the touched sublattice, 97% against 75% at random. (Ch. 11.6, 15.5.2)

**Clover:** The free cross-section of an opening: the circle minus three TiN crescents; 1013 nm² at the top support. (Ch. 1.2.3, 10.3)

**CN band:** Emission of the CN radical at 388.3 nm; the marker of nitrogen leaving the nitride. (Ch. 8.2)

**Cleared width:** The width of the middle-support opening in S2, set by the aperture image and the ion angular spread; 38.4 nm at 400 eV, 40.5 nm at 600 eV. (Ch. 10.6)

**Cluster (killer):** Three or more adjacent failed openings whose empty circle exceeds the reach of the dip. (Ch. 4.4)

**Crescent:** The overlap of an opening with a pillar: top face 317 nm², arc 38.2 nm, depth 15 nm at the top support. (Ch. 11.1)

---

## D

**d₀:** The steady-state polymer thickness on a non-consuming surface (TiN), 3.4 nm in the SN steps; the control variable of selectivity. (Ch. 3.3)

**Dip 1 / dip 2:** The two HF steps of the sequential route S2: the upper oxide (178 s) and the lower oxide (105 s). (Ch. 14.1)

**D_k:** Killer-defect density, in defects ≥ 154 nm per cm² of array; 0.02/cm² costs 0.6% die yield. (Ch. 4.5, 16.3)

**Door:** The opening as the only path to the pillars for every later process. (Ch. 1.2)

---

## E

**Empty circle (R_e):** The largest circle containing no working opening, for a given set of failed openings; 90, 103.9, 119.1, 155.9 nm for 1, 3 (triangle), 4 (rhombus), 7 failures. (Ch. 4.4.2)

**Endpoint (EP):** Detection of the clearing of a film from emission; falls 85% at SN1 and 25% at SN2. (Ch. 8)

**EWMA:** Exponentially weighted moving average control, λ = 0.3, limits ± 1.26σ. (Ch. 15.7.2)

---

## F

**FDC (fault detection and classification):** Monitoring of chamber signals per wafer. (Ch. 15.6)

**Fin:** The nitride sliver at the interface with a TiN pillar: 2.2 nm wide and 15 nm tall at the middle support (OE 40%); 3.7 nm and 45 nm at the top support (OE 25%). (Ch. 12.2–12.3)

**Flash (FL1, FL2):** A 4 s CH₃F/Ar plasma with no bias, before each nitride step, that builds 1.6 nm of polymer on TiN and removes 83% of the transient loss. (Ch. 3.5.3)

**Free area:** The area of the clover available to gases and liquids; falls to 20–28% of its etched value after ZAZ and to zero after the top electrode. (Ch. 1.2.3)

**Free span:** The length of a pillar between two supports once the oxide is removed; 650 nm between the top and middle supports at 1b. (Ch. 13.4)

---

## G

**Gaussian tail:** The probability that a site's clearing time exceeds the overetch time; 20.7σ away, so it does not produce not-open events. (Ch. 12.5.1)

---

## H

**HCN:** Hydrogen cyanide; formed in the nitride steps; requires point-of-use abatement and a monitor. (Ch. 3.2.3, 5.7.3)

**HFC (hydrofluorocarbon):** CH₂F₂, CH₃F, CHF₃; gases whose hydrogen removes nitrogen as HCN and builds polymer. (Ch. 3.2)

---

## I

**IEDF:** Ion energy distribution function; ± 50% at 2 MHz, ± 15% with a tailored waveform. (Ch. 5.2)

**Integral selectivity:** Nitride cleared (with overetch) divided by TiN lost; 93 for S0 and 226 for S1. (Ch. 3.5.4)

**Interface layer:** The 2–3 nm of oxynitride at each end of the middle support; the first and last nanometres of the nitride. (Ch. 2.3.2)

**Ion flush:** A 10 s ion-assisted O₂/Ar step at 100 eV that removes polymer at the bottom of the column. (Ch. 9.5.3)

---

## J

**Junction clamp:** The reverse-bias avalanche of the storage-node junction, about 10 V, that limits the potential of a charging pillar. (Ch. 13.1.1)

**Junction stress:** The dose I × t through the junction of a touched pillar: 0.14 C/cm² in S1. (Ch. 13.3.2)

---

## K

**K_hole, K_rough:** Stress concentration factors: 2 for an equibiaxial hole, 2.4 for a 1.5 nm edge notch with a 3 nm tip. (Ch. 4.6.1)

**Killer defect:** A defect of ≥ 154 nm that covers three adjacent openings. (Ch. 4.5)

---

## L

**LAND:** The last etch step, in C₄F₆/O₂/Ar, that lands 50–150 nm into the BPSG and supplies the nitride overetch of SN2 in the one-pass routes. (Ch. 3.6.2, 10.5.2)

**Layout A–E:** Five opening lattices: A (50 nm at 90 nm pitch, reference), B (56 at 90), C (44 at 78, rotated), D (50 at 78, rotated), E (64 at 135). (Ch. 4.2)

**Ligament:** The nitride between neighbouring openings; carries the net-section stress. (Ch. 4.6)

---

## M

**Micromask:** A particle, polymer plug, or resist defect on the floor of an opening that stops the etch; the cause of not-open events. (Ch. 12.5.2)

**Middle support:** The 50 nm nitride at 770–820 nm depth; opened by SN2 (or SN2 and LAND). (Ch. 1.1.2)

---

## N

**Not-open opening:** An opening whose nitride is not cut through; tolerated singly, fatal in clusters. (Ch. 4.4, 12.5)

---

## O

**OCD (optical critical dimension):** Scatterometry; ± 0.3 nm on the top CD and ± 1.5 nm on the middle CD of the lattice. (Ch. 15.2.2)

**OE (overetch):** 25% in SN1, 40% in SN2; sets the sliver width. (Ch. 12.3–12.4)

**OX:** The oxide step through the upper oxide (C₄F₆/O₂/Ar, 600 eV), pulsed in S1. (Ch. 5.4)

**Overlap depth:** The depth of the crescent into a pillar; 15 nm nominal, 20.2 nm at 3σ. (Ch. 4.3.2)

---

## P

**P (pre-opened lattice):** A scheme that opens the middle support before the upper oxide and pillars exist; not adopted because of hole-etch drift. (Ch. 14.6)

**PET (post-etch treatment):** The ion flush and radical strip that remove polymer and the ACL. (Ch. 9.5.3)

**Polymer-film model:** ER = R⁰ e^(−d_m/λ), d_m = (1 − c_m) d₀; consumption fractions 0.40 (SiN), 0.65 (SiO₂), 0 (TiN). (Ch. 3.3)

**PSG:** Phosphosilicate glass; the fast (300 nm/min) upper oxide of S2. (Ch. 14.2)

**Pull-in:** The collapse of free-standing neighbours under electrostatic load; 12.3 / 7.1 / 5.5 V for a 650 nm span (fixed, intermediate, pinned). (Ch. 13.4.2)

**Pulsing:** Modulating the bias at 10 kHz, 80% duty; relieves the oxide column but not the pillars. (Ch. 6.4, 13.2)

---

## R

**R_crit:** The reach of the HF dip from an opening centre: 95.4 nm at 105 s. (Ch. 4.4.1)

**Radical reach:** The depth to which a radical penetrates a column, λ = 1.155 r/√s; 12% of the dose at 770 nm for s = 0.005. (Ch. 7.2.3)

**Remote plasma:** A plasma generated away from the wafer that delivers neutrals only; used for the NF₃ trim. (Ch. 7.2)

---

## S

**S0:** Book #30's one-pass recipe: SN1, OX, SN2, LAND; 195 s, TiN loss 5.0 nm. (Ch. 1.7)

**S1:** The one-pass reference of this book: S0 plus a polymer flash before each nitride step and pulsed OX and LAND; 226 s, TiN loss 3.2 nm. (Ch. 1.7)

**S2 (S2A, S2D):** The sequential route; S2D touches every pillar (118 s, 1.8 nm), S2A reduces the ion flux to 16% (183 s, 1.3 nm). (Ch. 14.4)

**S3:** The sequential route for three supports. (Ch. 14.5)

**SiBN, SiCN:** Boron- and carbon-bearing nitrides; HF rates 0.15 and 0.25 nm/min, plasma rates 0.45 and 0.55 of SiN. (Ch. 2.5)

**SN1, SN2:** The nitride steps (top and middle support) in CH₂F₂/CF₄/O₂/Ar. (Ch. 3.2.2)

**Season:** A 10 s CH₃F/Ar deposition after a clean that removes the first-wafer effect. (Ch. 9.2.2)

**Seam:** The plane or line in the centre of a pillar where the TiN growth fronts met; open at the surface in 10% of pillars. (Ch. 11.4)

**Sliver:** See fin. (Ch. 12)

**Solid fraction:** The share of the support that is nitride after the opening: 39.2% (top), ≈ 46% (middle). (Ch. 1.1.3)

**Steady-state selectivity:** SiN:TiN with a fully grown film: 294. (Ch. 3.3.2)

**Stencil:** The top-support openings used as the mask for SN2 in S2. (Ch. 14.1)

---

## T

**Tailored waveform:** A bias waveform that narrows the IEDF to ± 15%; no benefit was found for it here. (Ch. 5.2.2, 6.3, 13.2.2)

**Taper:** The narrowing of the column with depth; 0.22° per side, 6 nm of CD to the middle support. (Ch. 10.1)

**TiFₓ skin:** The 1.5 nm self-limiting fluorinated layer on TiN; dissolves in HF and water. (Ch. 3.1, 7.2.4)

**TiN witness:** A blanket TiN pad in the scribe measured by XRF for the pillar-top loss. (Ch. 15.3)

**Top support:** The 120 nm nitride at the pillar tops; opened by SN1. (Ch. 1.1.2)

**Touched pillar:** A pillar overlapped by an opening; 75% of pillars. (Ch. 1.2.4)

**Transient (TiN loss):** The loss during the growth of the polymer film: 0.91 nm per step without a flash, 0.16 nm with one. (Ch. 3.5)

---

## U

**Untouched pillar:** A pillar that no opening overlaps; 25%. (Ch. 1.2.4, 11.5)

---

## V

**Virtual metrology (VM):** A model that predicts the TiN loss from FDC features; residual 0.12 nm. (Ch. 15.6.2)

**V_pull:** The voltage at which a free-standing span pulls in. (Ch. 13.4.2)

---

## W

**Waferless clean:** Per-wafer O₂/Ar clean with a cover wafer. (Ch. 9.2.1)

**Wetting:** Filling of a column by the liquid; requires polymer coverage ≤ 0.365 (θ ≤ 60°) at the bottom. (Ch. 9.4)

---

## Z

**ZAZ:** The ZrO₂/Al₂O₃/ZrO₂ dielectric of Book #33, 5.5 nm; coats every surface the etch leaves. (Ch. 1.2.3, 11.5.2)
